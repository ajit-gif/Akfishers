Frequently Asked Questions

How fresh is the seafood?

We select the catch fresh each day, clean it hygienically and pack it with ice before delivery. Orders are placed one day in advance so your fish is prepared fresh for your delivery slot.

Do you deliver to my area?

We deliver across Mumbai, including Thane and Navi Mumbai. Enter your pincode on the homepage to check availability and delivery charges.

Can I order in bulk?

Yes — we welcome bulk and advance orders for families, functions, festivals and restaurants. Call or WhatsApp us on +91 81081 06522 at least a day ahead.

What if I'm not home at delivery time?

Our delivery partner will call you before arrival. You can also reschedule your slot by calling us — we'll hold your order fresh on ice.
