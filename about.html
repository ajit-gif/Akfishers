Our Story

Born At The Sassoon Dock, Raised For Your Kitchen

AK Fishers Supplier LLP is a fresh seafood supplier committed to bringing quality fish and seafood directly from the sea to customers' doorsteps. The best seafood in Mumbai has always been locked behind crowded markets, unpredictable quality and hours of cleaning — and we set out to change that.

If it isn't fresh enough for our own family, it doesn't leave our facility.

We carefully select the fresh catch, clean it hygienically, pack it with care, and deliver it across Mumbai — for a fresh and reliable seafood experience every time.

Orders are taken one day in advance, and we welcome bulk orders for families, functions and events.
