Privacy Policy

How AK Fishers collects, uses and protects your information.

Last updated: August 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you use the AK Fishers website, we collect the information you provide directly: your name, mobile number, email address, delivery address and order details. We also collect basic usage data (pages visited, device type) to improve your experience.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process and deliver your seafood orders
  • To send order updates and delivery notifications via SMS/WhatsApp

3. Data Sharing

We never sell your personal data. We share only the minimum information required with delivery partners (name, address, phone) to fulfil your order, and with payment processors to complete transactions.

4. Data Security

Your data is stored securely and access is restricted to authorised staff. Payment details are processed by PCI-DSS compliant payment gateways — we never store your card or UPI details.
